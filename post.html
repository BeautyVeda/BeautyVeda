How to build a simple skincare routine you can actually follow

A beginner-friendly approach: keep the routine simple, consistent and suited to your everyday life.

Good skincare does not have to mean a shelf full of products. A simple routine is often easier to understand and easier to stick with.

1. Start with the basics

A gentle cleanser, a moisturizer and daytime sun protection are the basic building blocks for many people. Introduce products one at a time so you can understand how your skin responds.

2. Keep your morning routine short

On busy mornings, consistency matters more than complexity. Cleanse if needed, moisturize and use broad-spectrum sunscreen as directed on the product label.

3. Give your routine time

A routine is a habit, not an overnight transformation. Avoid changing several products at once and pay attention to irritation or discomfort.

BeautyVeda note: Everyone's skin is different. If you have persistent irritation or a skin condition, consider speaking with a qualified dermatologist.

4. Make it enjoyable

Keep your products organized, choose a routine that fits your schedule and focus on habits you can realistically maintain.
